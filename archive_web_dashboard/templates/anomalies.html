Anomaly Detection Methodology & Mathematical Models

Interquartile Range (IQR)

Calculates $Q_1$ (25th percentile) and $Q_3$ (75th percentile) for each facility. Outliers are identified when: $$\text{Reading} > Q_3 + (k \times \text{IQR})$$ where $k = 1.8$ for moderate, $k = 2.5$ for high, and $k = 3.5$ for critical severity.

Rolling Window Z-Score

Tracks a trailing 6-hour moving mean $\mu_{6h}$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{6h}$. Detects localized sudden surges where consumption jumps >75% above recent baselines, isolating machinery turn-ons.

Off-Hours Baseline Rules

Audits non-residential blocks (Academic, Computer Science, Canteen, Labs) between 01:00 and 05:00 AM. Any reading exceeding 135% of the annual median triggers an off-hours energy leakage alert.
