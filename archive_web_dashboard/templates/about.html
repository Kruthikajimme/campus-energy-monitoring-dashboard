ACADEMIC & ENTERPRISE ANALYTICS

Campus Energy Consumption Monitoring & Analytics Dashboard

Modern educational institutions operate complex electrical infrastructure spanning lecture halls, high-performance computing centers, experimental research laboratories, student residences, and dining commons. This web-based analytics platform provides university facility administrators and sustainability committees with real-time visibility into building-level energy intensity, peak demand charges, carbon emissions, and operational anomalies.

Python 3.12 Flask Microframework Pandas & NumPy Chart.js 4 OpenPyXL Bootstrap 5.3
Core Project Objectives
  • • Monitor multi-facility energy telemetry
  • • Identify highest and lowest consumers
  • • Pinpoint campus peak power demand
  • • Detect statistical consumption anomalies
  • • Uncover after-hours electricity leakage
  • • Support data-driven green campus initiatives

Modular Application Architecture

Separation of concerns across data simulation, cleaning, analytics, and reporting
1. Synthetic Generator & Cleaning

modules/data_generator.py & modules/data_cleaning.py

Generates reproducible 90-day hourly telemetry for 7 campus facilities with synthetic noise (missing fields, duplicates, negative values, and sensor spikes). Cleans and imputes using hourly medians while logging audit decisions to JSON.

2. Analytics & Anomaly Detection

modules/analytics.py & modules/anomaly_detection.py

Computes multi-dimensional slice-and-dice aggregations, Pearson correlations between occupancy and load, and statistical anomaly detection using Interquartile Range (IQR) and rolling 6-hour moving Z-scores.

3. Automated Insights & Excel Reports

modules/insights.py & modules/report_generator.py

Synthesizes key findings and energy conservation measures (peak shaving, chiller setbacks, solar expansion). Generates 5-sheet corporate-grade Excel workbooks with embedded native charts via OpenPyXL.

Original Implementation Statement: In accordance with academic integrity and ethical engineering standards, our codebase represents an entirely original full-stack implementation. Our system features an independently developed synthetic dataset generation engine, custom Pandas cleaning and imputation pipeline, dual-statistical anomaly detector (IQR & rolling Z-score), modern responsive dashboard theme, and dynamic OpenPyXL Excel reporting engine.

SIMULATED DATASET NOTICE: All electricity readings, peak power demand values, voltage metrics, and ambient temperatures displayed across this application and exported reports are synthetically generated using a fixed random seed (42) for academic demonstration and software prototyping. They should not be interpreted as physical hardware measurements of any real-world university facility.
